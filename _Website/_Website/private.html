Frequent Questions

How far in advance should I book? expand_more
We recommend booking at least 4-6 weeks in advance for weekend events and 2 weeks for weekday corporate luncheons. However, do contact us for last-minute availability.
Can you accommodate severe allergies? expand_more
Absolutely. Our culinary team is highly experienced in preparing bespoke menus for all dietary requirements, including vegan, gluten-free, and severe nut allergies.
Is there a minimum spend? expand_more
Minimum spend varies by room selection and date. Our event liaisons will provide a detailed quote once we understand your guest count and requirements.
